# 5.4 Crash recovery

Goal: the switch can die at any point and a new process finishes every payment correctly. Two switch processes can run at once.

## Build

- Lease and fencing token on `txns`:
  - `acquireLease` succeeds only if the row is unleased or its lease has expired, and it increments `fence`.
  - Every step-log insert, step-log finish, transition and update requires `fence = ? AND lease_owner = ?`.
  - A process that lost its lease gets `LeaseLost` and stops.
- The recovery loop (`src/recovery.ts`, every `SWITCH_RECOVERY_INTERVAL_MS`) drives non-terminal rows whose `next_run_at` is due and whose lease is free or has expired.
- A STARTED row with no result, left behind by a crash, counts as "may have happened". The retry sends the identical request, and the adapter replays its stored answer.
- Chaos without Docker:
  - `SWITCH_CHAOS=true` exposes `PUT /internal/chaos { crashAt, txnId? }`.
  - Crash points are `<step>:before-call`, `<step>:after-call` and `after:<STATE>`.
  - The process exits with code 137, and `scripts/stack.ts` (`restartOnCrash`) restarts it.

## Tests

- `test/recovery.test.ts`:
  - A crash at each of 9 happy-path boundaries, 5 reversal-path boundaries, and during a credit reversal.
  - Three crashes in a row.
  - Lease exclusion.
  - A zombie that wakes up after being fenced out.
  - Two engines on two SQLite connections racing over 40 payments while faults are injected.
- `tests/e2e/phase-5.4.test.ts`:
  - The real process is killed at 5 boundaries, on the reversal path, and on a dropped response followed by a crash.
  - A second real switch process runs on the same `switch.db` while the first one is killed during load.
