# 5.3 Resilience

Goal: handle slow banks, dead banks and lying networks without guessing.

## Build

- Classification in `src/classify.ts` (the fixed table):
  - 400, 404, 409 and 422 are BUSINESS. The exception is `409 ADAPTER_REQUEST_MISMATCH`, which is MISMATCH.
  - 502, 503 and connection refused are TRANSIENT.
  - 504, a client timeout, a dropped socket and any other 5xx are UNKNOWN.
- Retries with full jitter for every adapter step, since the adapter is idempotent (`src/resilience.ts` `backoffMs`).
- Circuit breaker per bank (CLOSED, OPEN, then HALF_OPEN with a single probe) and a bulkhead per bank. A rejected call is recorded as TRANSIENT with code `CIRCUIT_OPEN` or `BULKHEAD_FULL`, and the bank is not called.
- When the retries run out:
  - If any attempt may have happened, the payment goes DEEMED.
  - Otherwise a failed hold ends DEBIT_FAILED and a failed credit ends CREDIT_FAILED. Capture, release and reverse are parked with backoff instead.
- DEEMED is handled by the recovery loop. It calls `GET /banks/:bank/status/:txnId`:
  - DONE continues forward.
  - FAILED takes the business branch.
  - Missing or IN_PROGRESS re-sends the identical request.
  - After `SWITCH_DEEMED_MAX_POLLS` attempts the payment goes to MANUAL_REVIEW.

- `test/units.test.ts`: retry policy, breaker, bulkhead, classification.
- `test/http-bank.test.ts`: real sockets for a drop, a timeout, a refused connection, a 503 and a 422.
- `test/saga.test.ts`: transient retry, timeout after commit, dropped response, bank down on hold or credit, DEEMED resolved as DONE, FAILED and missing, DEEMED unresolved, parked capture, breaker fail-fast and recovery, refused connection, compensation retry.
